# Story-to-Shorts pack — Why products get views but no sales

Angle: mistake · ~130 words · ~52s at 150 wpm

## Script (feed this to the Talking-Head pipeline)

Stop. You're making the one mistake that kills why products get views but no sales — and it's not what you think. A real store we run has eighty-six digital products live right now. Last week it crossed one hundred views and forty-seven clicks. The number of orders was zero. But here is where it gets interesting. The products were not the problem, because the same files sell the story in their free demos. The page was the problem: it never said who the bundle was for, what exactly was inside, or why the bundle beats buying the parts. One rewrite later, a five-buyer panel scored the page ninety out of one hundred instead of fifty. Agree or not? Tell me in the comments — and follow for part two.
